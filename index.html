The Lost School Colours

One sunny morning, Momo the Monster and Bolt the Robot found a mysterious school bag. Inside were crayons, pencils, a notebook, an eraser, a ruler—and colourful words whose letters had jumped all over the place!

We need a clever Spelling Explorer!” said Bolt. Each correct word gives our academy a bright star. Collect three stars, unlock a new story, and help the school colours shine again. Are you ready?
